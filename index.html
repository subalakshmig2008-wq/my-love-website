FROM MY HEART

One Thing I Wanted To Tell You... 💌

Heyyy loosu… ❤️ Enna sollrathunu therila… Aana romba naala en mind-la irukura oru vishayatha innikku un kitta sollanum nu thonuchu. First time unna paatha appo, nee en life-la ivlo important person ah maaruva nu naan nenachave illa. Konjam konjama un kooda pesurathu, un kooda spend panna time, unoda chinna chinna things ellame enakku romba pidikka aarambichiduchu. 🥹❤️ Sometimes namma rendu perum pesama irukalam… Sometimes misunderstandings varalam… Sometimes life-la enna nadakkumnu namakkey theriyama irukalam… Aana onnu mattum true… Naan unna love panradhu mattum maaradhu. ❤️ Un kooda irukkumbodhu naan happy-ah feel panren. Un kitta edhavadhu share pannanum nu thonum. Oru naal full-ah pesama irundhaalum, somehow unna pathi yosichite iruppen. 😭 Nee perfect person nu naan sollala… Naanum perfect illa. Aana un imperfections kooda accept pannitu, un kooda irukanum nu dhaan aasapadren. ❤️ Unakku life-la happy moments varumbodhu, adha un kooda celebrate panna naan irukanum. Unakku kashtama irukkumbodhu, “Enna aachu?” nu kekka oru aal ah naan irukanum. Nee tired-ah irukkumbodhu konjam pesi sirikka vaikka naan irukanum. Nee edhavadhu achieve pannumbodhu, “Naan sonnen la, nee kandippa pannuva nu” nu proud-ah solla naan irukanum. 🫶🏻 And honestly… Un life-la naan oru temporary person-ah irukka virumbala. Unakku konjam distance venumna naan respect pannuven. Unakku time venumna naan wait pannuven. Aana en heart-la irukkuradha sollama irukka mudiyala… I LOVE YOU. ❤️ And yes… I want you. Not just for today… Not just for a few months… I want to see where this life takes us together. 🥹❤️
